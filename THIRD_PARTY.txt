5. GALAHAD GLRT (optional Python distribution: galahad-optrove)
   Source: https://github.com/ralna/GALAHAD
   Documentation: https://ralna.github.io/galahad_docs/html/Python/glrt.html
   License: BSD-3-Clause (upstream library and interface retain their notices)
   Use: Established Lanczos solver for MF cubic Newton subproblems. The local
   adapter calls the installed library; it does not copy the solver source.
   Method reference: Cartis, Gould and Toint (2011), Adaptive cubic
   regularisation methods for unconstrained optimization. Part I: motivation,
   convergence and numerical results. Mathematical Programming 127, 245-295.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-009-0286-5 (Section 6.2).
