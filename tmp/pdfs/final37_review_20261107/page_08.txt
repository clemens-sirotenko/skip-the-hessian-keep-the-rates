4.4 Faster rates in the finite-dimensionalC 2 setting
Finally, we consider the finite-dimensional locally C 2 case, where the local strong convexity as-
sumption in Assumption 2 can be weakened to local convexity. While C 2-regularity is standard in
Newton methods, existing results do not yield improved asymptotic rates. Here, local C 2-smoothness
implies ηk →0 in (15), hence λk →0 by Proposition 4.1, which together with Theorem 3.4 yields
superlinear convergence. The formal statement, proved in Section D.5, is as follows.
Theorem 4.9(Main theorem, finite dimensions, C 2).Let dim(H)<∞ , Assumption 1 hold, F
satisfy the Polyak–Łojasiewicz condition (10), p <1, and the sublevel set F0 be bounded. Then,
F(x k)−F ∗ →0 , xk →x ∗, and ∥F ′(xk)∥∗ →0 linearly as k→ ∞. If additionally f is locally
C 2 and locally convex aroundx ∗, andH :=∇ 2f, then the above convergences are all superlinear.
