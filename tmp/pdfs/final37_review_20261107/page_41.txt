According to the above theorem, the whole convergence picture is as follows. From any starting
point, the method converges linearly to a solution x∗ thanks to the PL condition. Since the linear

the method starts to converge superlinearly when it is sufficiently small. In that sense, the superlinear
convergence is local and asymptotic, but the linear rate is global and nonasymptotic.
