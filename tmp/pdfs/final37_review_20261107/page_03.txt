2 Semismooth Newton Method
We start by making the following standard assumption regarding the problem (P).
Assumption 1.(i) The function f:H →R is continuously Fr´echet differentiable and f ′ is

2 -Lipschitz.
(ii) The functionψ:H → Ris convex, proper, and lower semicontinuous.
(iii) The function F :=f+ψ is bounded from below by the global minimal value F ∗ and
problem (P) has a nonempty solution setS :={x ∗ ∈ H:F(x∗) =F ∗}.
(iv) Given a point x∈ H, we can evaluate a linear self-adjoint operator 2 H(x) such that the
operator norm is bounded, i.e., ∥H(x)∥ op := suph∈H,h̸=0 ∥H(x)h∥ ∗∥h∥ ≤ L2 for all x∈domf .
Throughout, ∂F(x) denotes the limiting subdifferential of F , which under the above assumption
coincides with the Fr´echet subdifferential [66, 50]. We writeF ′(x)∈∂F(x).
