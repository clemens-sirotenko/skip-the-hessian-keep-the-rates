Optimization and parameters.We apply Algorithm 1 to the objective in (26) with penalty
parameter λ= 1.0 and ψ≡0 , so that the proximal subproblem reduces to solving a linear system.
The Hessian is computed via automatic differentiation in PyTorch. At each inner iteration, we solve
a damped Newton system of the form (H+λ kI)s=g . Because the objective is nonconvex, H
may be indefinite, and H+λ kI can be ill-conditioned or singular; in such cases, we fall back to a
least-squares solve. In this experiment, we symmetrize the Hessian as 12 (H+H ⊤) before solvingthe linear system. The damped Newton systems are solved by LU factorization, with a least-squares
fallback when necessary.
As a baseline, we minimize the same objective (26) using PyTorch’s 4 Adamoptimizer with default
momentum parameters and no weight decay. We run T= 10 4 full-batch optimization steps. The
learning rate is initialized to η= 7×10 −4 and adapted using ReduceLROnPlateau (factor 0.5,
patience 5, threshold 10−5, and minimum learning rate10−6). In both algorithms, the Hessian penalty
is computed as described above using K= 10 power iterations with a deterministic initialization.
