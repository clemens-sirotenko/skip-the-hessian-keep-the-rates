E.2 Non-negative matrix factorization
Semismooth differentiability.The smooth quartic data-fit part of (27) is a polynomial in the
entries of (U, V)and hence C ∞. To show semismooth differentiability of the whole objective it
remains to show that U7→ ∥(−U)+∥2F =∥U −∥2F is C 1 with semismooth gradient. The continuous
differentiability directly follows from the differentiability of t7→max{−t,0} 2. Its derivative is the
piecewise affine map t7→2 min{t,0}. Piecewise affine mappings are strongly semismooth (i.e.,
1-order semismooth), cf. [23]. Hence the objective gradient isγ-order semismooth withγ= 1.
