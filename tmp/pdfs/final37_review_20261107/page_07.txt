Assumption (18) is nonrestrictive since it can be guaranteed at iteration k= 0 by a burn-in phase of

4.3 Faster rate underγ-order semismoothness
In this section, we impose a stronger regularity assumption standard in the SSN literature; cf. [73, 3].
Assumption 4. f ′ is γ-order semismooth at x∗ for some γ∈(0,1] with respect to H, i.e., as d→0 ,
∥f ′(x∗ +d)−f ′(x∗)−H(x ∗ +d)d∥ ∗ =O(∥d∥ 1+γ).(23)
Higher-order semismoothness is the key regularity behind accelerated local convergence of semis-
mooth Newton methods, and it dovetails with our gradient regularization framework. It is satisfied
by many ML primitives, including piecewise-affine maps such as the ReLU activation, Euclidean
projections onto polyhedral sets, and spectral thresholding/projection operators (e.g., projection onto
the PSD cone) [69, 14]. We further highlight residual maps arising in kernel optimal transport as a
recent application [43] and the classical squared hinge loss in SVM.
